# Our Core Values

create an artifact for the following :


I really liked Amazon's values. In the beginning of the book itself, they mention that the HR leadership went ahead and interviewed leadership people at Amazon and figured out the top nine values, and ended up being 14 values that they've brainstormed upon. And then I really feel that these are some good values that any new company or a new startup should frame for themselves. I want to do this exercise for Hockey.ai, where we are growing very fast. We are now a 30-member team. I wanted to invite people to share the values that they want to take from the old office to the new office and they think will help us take the company to the next stage. Can you create a simple web page explaining the concept of creating these values, the way Amazon did? Give the list of values that Amazon had, suggest the values that Hockey can have, and give some sort of question or an invitation for people to think about these values that they can submit over a hard paper or a paper sheet that will be given to them towards the end of the week for them to think over the week what should be these values, and then we will try to vote for the best values out of the ones submitted and try to figure out which values makes most sense to all of us.
